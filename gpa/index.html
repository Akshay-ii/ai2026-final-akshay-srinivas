Ilia State University — GPA Calculation Rule

At Ilia State University, GPA (Grade Point Average) is the sum of the products of each course's Grade Point (GP) and its credit count, divided by the total credits attempted:

GPA = Σ (GP of course × credits of course) / Σ (credits of course)

What is GP?

GP is the score received in a course, converted onto a 4.0 scale.

Within the E range (51–60), GP is frozen at 1.00. From 61 to 95, GP rises evenly in increments of 1/12 (≈0.08) per point. Within the A+ range (96–100), GP is frozen at 4.00. Below 51, GP is 0 (Fail).

The frozen GP at the E and A+ extremes reflects that differences between scores inside those ranges aren't considered meaningful — any score from 51–60 is equally a bare pass grade, and any score from 96–100 is equally top marks.

Other rules

GPA is issued for all academic programs except doctoral studies and certificate programs. Only the final GPA is issued, shown on the diploma supplement (this does not apply to the one-tier Diploma in Medicine program). Final GPA is rounded to one decimal place.
